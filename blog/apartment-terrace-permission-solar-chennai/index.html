Apartment Permission for Solar Panel Installation in Chennai

Buying guide · 26 November 2026 · 6 min read

Solar panel installation in Chennai on an independent house uses a roof the owner controls. A flat is different. The common terrace belongs to the association, and a plant that starts without that consent gets stopped at the first complaint.

What the letter has to say

The association, or every owner who uses that roof, should say in writing that this flat may place modules on a marked part of the terrace. It should name who pays if a pipe is disturbed, and that the structure is removed if the society later needs that slab. A verbal “go ahead” from the secretary is not enough for solar panel installation in Madipakkam blocks.

Roofs that should wait

A balcony rail is not a terrace. A roof already covered by one owner’s tank and drying lines, with no clear patch, is not a plant. A large apartment block with a locked terrace door is not a weekend job. The honest answer is to wait until the society has a roof policy.

The EB connection must be the flat’s. The plant follows the meter named on the bill. It does not follow a neighbour’s connection because that terrace corner is free.

Independent houses are simpler

If the sale deed and the EB bill are in your name, and the terrace is yours, solar panel installation in Chennai does not need an association letter. Bring the bill to the free site inspection. Shared roofs should bring the letter before anyone drills.
