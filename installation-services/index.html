Installation services only. Panels, inverters, cables, and mounting structures are supplied by you unless otherwise agreed. Residential installation from ₹3,000 per kW.

Civil work

  • Foundation work for ground-mounted structures
  • RCC pedestals where required
  • Roof drilling, anchors, and waterproof sealing
  • Base preparation, alignment, and levelling

Mounting and panels

  • Assemble rails and clamps
  • Align panels and torque fasteners
  • String configuration and MC4 connectors
  • Verify roof safety

Inverter and electrical

  • Wall mounting with ventilation clearance
  • AC/DC termination and cable dressing
  • Earthing and lightning connections if the hardware is on site
  • Junction box, MCB, and isolator connections

Testing

  • Voltage, polarity, insulation resistance, and earthing tests
  • Inverter startup and performance testing
  • Mobile monitoring setup where the inverter supports it
