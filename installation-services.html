Complete installation service

From structure work to successful switch-on.

Residential installation starts at ₹3,000 per kW, including the essential labour, civil work, installation, wiring, testing, and commissioning work.

Civil work
  • Foundation work for ground-mounted structures
  • RCC pedestal construction where required
  • Roof drilling, anchor fixing, and waterproof sealing
  • Mounting-base preparation, alignment, and levelling
Mounting structure
  • Assemble mounting structure, rails, and clamps
  • Secure all fasteners and align panels
  • Verify roof safety
Solar panel installation
  • Panel mounting and alignment for maximum generation
  • Torque tightening to manufacturer standards
  • String configuration and MC4 connector installation
Inverter installation
  • Wall mounting and ventilation-clearance verification
  • AC/DC cable termination
  • Configuration, commissioning, and startup testing
Electrical installation
  • AC and DC wiring with cable dressing
  • Earthing and lightning-protection connections, if available
  • Junction-box, MCB, and isolator connections
Testing & commissioning
  • Voltage, polarity, insulation-resistance, and earthing tests
  • Inverter startup and performance testing
  • Mobile monitoring setup where supported
