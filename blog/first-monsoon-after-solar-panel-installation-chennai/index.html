First Monsoon After Solar Panel Installation in Chennai: What to Check

Maintenance · 22 November 2026 · 6 min read

The first northeast monsoon after solar panel installation in Chennai is when leaks, loose glands, and silt show up. Generation will dip on grey days; that alone is not a fault. Water where it should not be is.

Expect lower units on overcast days

October to December brings cloud and rain. A 3 kW plant that made strong summer numbers will look quieter on the monitoring app. Compare similar weather days, not a bright April afternoon with a November drizzle. Heavy rain and generation explains the seasonal pattern.

Walk the roof after the heaviest spells

Check roof penetrations and parapet flashings for damp patches below. Look at cable glands on the inverter and array ends for silt or drips. Bird mesh should still sit tight. You do not need to pressure-wash the glass after every shower — rain helps loose dust; baked droppings still need a proper clean later.

Inverter and isolators in driving rain

If the inverter was mounted in a splash path, monsoon weeks expose it. Fans may run less on cool wet days; that is normal. Persistent fault lights, water inside a cover, or a dead display are not. Inverter placement is harder to fix after the fact, so report issues early while labour warranty still applies.

For solar panel installation in Madipakkam: marsh-edge humidity and road dust leave a film even after rain. Book a post-monsoon clean rather than waiting for summer. Cleaning frequency gives a practical schedule.
