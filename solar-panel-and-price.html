Two different jobs

Owning the modules and fitting them are not the same bill.

If you already bought panels and an inverter, fitting starts at ₹3,000 per kW. That is labour: civil work, mounting, wiring, testing, and commissioning. The modules, inverter, cables, and structure steel stay yours. Details are on installation services.

A complete plant is the other price. We specify the modules, bring them, and commission the system. That figure moves with roof height, shade, and whether the modules are DCR. It is not a city-wide rupee-per-watt printed on a banner.

What changes the figure

Four lines that move a home quote.

01

Units, not appliances

Monthly consumption and clear roof set the kilowatts. Two air conditioners are not a 2 kW plant.

02

Structure height

A low clamp-down array is cheaper and can block the terrace. An elevated frame costs more and keeps the floor usable.

03

DCR or NDCR

NDCR modules are often cheaper per watt. They drop the subsidy. DCR keeps that path open. See NDCR vs DCR.

04

Paperwork

Net-meter and subsidy files are part of a serious quote. A low module price that ignores them delays the lower bill.

Subsidy

The assistance can be larger than the module gap.

Eligible homeowners can receive a government subsidy of up to ₹78,000. Amount and approval follow current scheme rules. Compare the price you pay after that assistance, not the module line alone. If one quote is silent on who files the papers, it is not the same product as a quote that names the work.

Questions, answered

Before you treat a rate as the price.

Why is there no rate per panel here?

A module rate leaves out roof, shade, structure, inverter, and who supplies the equipment. The plant price is written after a free visit.

What does ₹3,000 per kW cover?

Labour-only fitting when you already own the panels and inverter. Modules, inverter, cables, and structure materials are excluded.

How does the subsidy change what you pay?

Eligible homes can receive up to ₹78,000 with DCR modules and an approved file. A cheaper NDCR module can cost more once that assistance is given up.

What should two quotes show?

Wattage, module count, DCR or NDCR, roof area, exclusions, and who files the net meter and subsidy.

A free visit from the Madipakkam team. The quote names what is included before any work starts.
