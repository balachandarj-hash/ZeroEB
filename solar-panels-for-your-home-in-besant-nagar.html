What these roofs actually do

Shade that moves, and air that salts the steel.

Many homes sit on the numbered avenues, a short walk from Elliot's Beach. A neem or a rain tree in the compound can cover part of the terrace by late afternoon, then lift as the sun drops. That is a different problem from a neighbour’s wall, which stays in one place all year. We mark the hours the roof is actually clear before anyone picks a wattage.

The city-wide version of this choice is panels for a Chennai home. This page is only the Besant Nagar constraints: tree shade, salt off the beach, and a team that can come back after switch-on from Madipakkam.

What we check on site

Four things that change the module line.

01

Clear hours

Trees move. We note which part of the terrace stays free from late morning through the hot hours, and we leave a walkway around the tank and the stair head.

02

Beach air

Homes nearer Elliot's Beach need frames, clamps, and cable glands specified for salty air. That line belongs on the quote beside the wattage.

03

Daytime use

The bill shows what the house draws while the sun is up. Modules are counted to that use, not to a package size from a brochure.

04

DCR

Subsidy plants need cells and modules made in India. Eligible homes can receive up to ₹78,000. Amount and approval follow current scheme rules.

Who it fits

A terrace you control, not a balcony facing the beach.

Independent houses on the avenues are the usual fit, along with a top-floor terrace when the association has allowed the roof to be used. A shared roof with no written permission is not a plant we will start. A balcony rail, even one with a sea view, is not a plant either. Flats without terrace access need a different conversation, and often the honest answer is to wait.

If the modules are already in a godown, fitting is labour and is quoted on installation services. How the price should be read, including the subsidy gap, is on panel and price. NDCR versus DCR is in that guide before you lock a cheaper import.

Questions, answered

Before you order from a photograph.

Do you visit Besant Nagar from Madipakkam?

Yes. The survey is free and does not commit you. Call or send the bill and a terrace photo if you have one.

Will the trees force a smaller plant?

Often they will. We size to the hours that stay clear, not to the full outline of the roof. A photo taken at noon can hide the afternoon shade.

Does beach air change the specification?

Yes, especially nearer Elliot's Beach. Ask for frames, clamps, and glands suited to salty air, not only a higher wattage sticker.

Do I need DCR modules?

Yes, if you want up to ₹78,000 in central assistance. NDCR is only if you have chosen to give that subsidy up.
