Complete preventive care

What’s included in our Solar Maintenance Service.

Our AMC is a detailed system care programme—not just a panel-cleaning visit. Open each check to see what our technicians inspect.

1. Solar panel cleaning
  • Foam wash using a soft, non-abrasive cleaning solution
  • Remove dust, bird droppings, leaves, and debris
  • Clean panel surfaces with purified water
  • Spot-clean stubborn stains and complete a final visual inspection
2. Solar panel health inspection
  • Inspect for cracks, broken glass, and discoloration / hot spots
  • Check the panel frame condition and mounting stability
  • Check for moisture or water ingress
  • Inspect the backsheet for damage
3. Mounting structure inspection
  • Tighten loose nuts and bolts
  • Inspect rails for corrosion and roof anchor points
  • Verify overall structural stability
  • Inspect for rust; repainting is available as an optional service
4. DC cable inspection
  • Check cable insulation and UV damage
  • Verify cable routing and tighten cable ties
  • Check MC4 connectors
  • Replace damaged connectors if required
5. Earthing system verification
  • Check earth-pit connections and earthing continuity
  • Inspect earth-wire condition and tighten terminals
  • Measure earth resistance where applicable
6. Lightning protection inspection
  • Inspect the lightning arrestor
  • Verify grounding connection and physical condition
  • Tighten all connections
7. Inverter health check
  • Clean the inverter exterior, cooling fans, and ventilation area
  • Review inverter error logs and firmware version
  • Confirm normal operation and test emergency shutdown where applicable
8. Electrical safety inspection
  • Inspect AC/DC isolators, MCBs, breakers, and SPD
  • Tighten electrical terminals
  • Check for overheating marks and inspect the distribution board
9. Performance testing
  • Measure system voltage and current output
  • Verify inverter efficiency against expected performance
  • Identify underperforming strings and abnormal power loss
10. Monitoring system verification
  • Check Wi-Fi connectivity and mobile-app monitoring
  • Confirm live data reporting
  • Test alerts and notifications
  • Ensure online monitoring is functioning properly
11. Roof safety inspection
  • Check mounting points and roof waterproofing near brackets
  • Verify no water leakage
  • Inspect cable entry points and safe access pathways
12. Vegetation & shading assessment
  • Identify new shading from trees or nearby obstructions
  • Recommend trimming where required
  • Verify optimal sunlight exposure
13. Bird protection inspection
  • Check bird-mesh condition and remove nesting material
  • Clean bird droppings beneath panels
  • Inspect for pest-related damage
14. System documentation
  • Record maintenance activities and inspection findings
  • Capture before-and-after photos
  • Document power generation and recommendations
15. Customer report
  • Cleaning completed and system health status
  • Energy-generation performance and safety-inspection results
  • Recommended repairs, if any, and technician observations
  • Before-and-after photos

Complete installation service

Everything needed to safely bring your solar system online.

Residential installation starts at ₹3,000 per kW and includes the essential labour, civil work, installation, wiring, testing, and commissioning work.

Civil work
  • Foundation work for ground-mounted structures
  • RCC pedestal construction where required
  • Roof drilling, anchor fixing, and waterproof sealing
  • Mounting-base preparation, alignment, and levelling
Mounting structure installation
  • Assemble mounting structure, rails, and clamps
  • Secure all fasteners and align panels
  • Verify roof safety
Solar panel installation
  • Panel mounting and alignment for maximum generation
  • Torque tightening to manufacturer standards
  • String configuration and MC4 connector installation
Inverter installation
  • Wall mounting and ventilation-clearance verification
  • AC/DC cable termination
  • Configuration, commissioning, and startup testing
Electrical installation
  • AC and DC wiring with cable dressing
  • Earthing installation and lightning-protection connections, if available
  • Junction-box, MCB, and isolator connections
Testing & commissioning
  • Voltage, polarity, insulation-resistance, and earthing tests
  • Inverter startup and performance testing
  • Mobile monitoring setup where supported

Questions, answered

Solar without the guesswork.

How do I know what system size I need?

We review your electricity consumption, roof space, shading, and future energy needs during a site survey to recommend a suitable plant size.

Will solar work during Chennai’s monsoon season?

Solar production varies with sunlight, but a well-designed system continues to generate power throughout the year. We design with local conditions in mind.

How long does a residential installation take?

Timing depends on system size and site conditions. After the survey and approvals, most residential installations can be completed efficiently by our team.

Do you provide support after installation?

Yes. ZeroEB is available for guidance, system care, and maintenance support once your plant is operating.
