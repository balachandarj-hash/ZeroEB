Questions

Before you book the visit.

How do I know what system size I need?+

We review your electricity consumption, roof space, shading, and future energy needs during a site survey. Capacity comes from the units you use while the sun is up, not from counting air conditioners.

Will solar work during Chennai’s monsoon season?+

Production varies with sunlight, but a well-designed system continues to generate through the year. We size the plant with local weather, heat, and shade in mind.

How long does a residential installation take?+

Timing depends on system size, roof condition, and approvals. After the survey, most residential plants are installed and commissioned by the same local team.

Do I lose my EB connection?+

No. A typical home plant is grid-tied. Daytime use is served by the panels first. After sunset, and during a power cut on an on-grid inverter, the EB line still supplies the home.

Do you provide support after installation?+

Yes. ZeroEB stays available for guidance, panel cleaning, and maintenance once the plant is operating. The office is in Madipakkam.
