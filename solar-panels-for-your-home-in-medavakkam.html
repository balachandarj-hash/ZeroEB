The question that comes first

Is this the last floor?

On the layouts off Medavakkam Main Road, a ground floor is often occupied while the terrace is still a building site in the owner's mind. Exposed pipes, a parapet that is only half built, or a tank sitting on a temporary pad are signs the roof is not finished. We do not anchor rails to that slab.

When you have decided there will be no further floor, the parapet has to be complete and the tank has to be in its final place. Modules then take the open tiled area that remains, sized to the daytime bill. City-wide module choice is on panels for a Chennai home. Wind off the marsh is the Pallikaranai case. A garden terrace beside our counter is the Madipakkam case. Here the first limit is whether the roof is final.
