While you still live there

The roof is a workplace for a few days, then a floor again.

A house is not an empty commercial slab. Clothes dry there, the tank is filled from there, and someone walks up in the evening. We mark those paths before the steel is cut. On most terraces the array is raised so that life returns underneath once the crew leaves.

The visit itself is free and does not commit you. Bring a recent electricity bill if you have one. Shade from a stair cover or the next building decides more than a catalogue of module brands.

Questions, answered

Before the crew is on your stair.

Can we use the terrace during the work?

It is a work site for those days, so tank access and drying are planned around the structure. Afterwards most arrays are raised and the floor is usable again.

Does the EB connection stay?

Yes. The roof covers daytime use, the grid covers the night, and a net meter records both.

How long is the house a work site?

A few working days once materials arrive. Net-meter and subsidy files follow TANGEDCO, which is a separate wait.

What should we keep ready?

A recent bill, terrace access, and a note of shade. Photos help. They are not required before the first call.
