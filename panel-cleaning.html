Complete preventive care

What’s included in our Solar Maintenance Service.

Our AMC is a detailed system-care programme—not just a panel-cleaning visit. Open each check to see what our technicians inspect.

1. Solar panel cleaning
  • Foam wash with non-abrasive solution and purified water
  • Remove dust, bird droppings, leaves, and debris
  • Spot-clean stains and complete final visual inspection
2. Panel health inspection
  • Inspect for cracks, broken glass, and hot spots
  • Check panel frame, mounting stability, and moisture ingress
  • Inspect backsheet condition
3. Mounting structure
  • Tighten loose nuts and bolts
  • Inspect rails, anchors, corrosion, and stability
  • Rust inspection; repainting available as an optional service
4. DC cable inspection
  • Check insulation, UV damage, and cable routing
  • Tighten cable ties and inspect MC4 connectors
  • Replace damaged connectors if required
5. Earthing verification
  • Check earth-pit connections and continuity
  • Inspect earth-wire condition and tighten terminals
  • Measure earth resistance where applicable
6. Lightning protection
  • Inspect lightning arrestor and grounding connection
  • Check physical condition and tighten connections
7. Inverter health check
  • Clean inverter exterior, cooling fans, and ventilation
  • Review error logs and firmware version
  • Confirm normal operation and shutdown operation where applicable
8. Electrical safety
  • Inspect AC/DC isolators, MCBs, breakers, and SPD
  • Tighten terminals and check for overheating marks
  • Inspect distribution board
9. Performance testing
  • Measure voltage and current output
  • Verify inverter efficiency and expected performance
  • Identify underperforming strings or power loss
10. Monitoring verification
  • Check Wi-Fi and mobile-app monitoring
  • Confirm live-data reporting, alerts, and notifications
11. Roof safety
  • Inspect mounting points and waterproofing near brackets
  • Check cable entries, leaks, and safe access paths
12. Shading assessment
  • Identify new shade from trees and obstructions
  • Recommend trimming where required
  • Verify optimal sunlight exposure
13. Bird protection
  • Check bird-mesh condition and remove nesting material
  • Clean droppings beneath panels and inspect pest damage
14. Documentation
  • Record maintenance activities and findings
  • Capture before-and-after photos
  • Document power generation and recommendations
15. Customer report
  • Cleaning completion, health status, and energy performance
  • Safety results, recommended repairs, and observations
  • Before-and-after photos
