Solar installation in Chennai: what to expect before you sign.

Buying guide · 21 September 2026 · 8 min read

Solar installation in Chennai is a crowded search. National brands, aggregators, and neighbourhood crews all appear in the same results. The useful filter is not the biggest advertisement. It is whether the quote describes your roof, your TANGEDCO connection, and the work that still happens after the last panel is clamped.

Chennai weather is part of the design

This city is excellent for generation and hard on equipment if the install is sloppy. Heat reduces panel output at midday. Coastal humidity and monsoon rain punish poor earthing, open cable glands, and unsealed roof penetrations. Dust and bird droppings sit on the glass through the dry months and can cost noticeable units until someone cleans the array.

A system designed for solar installation in Chennai should assume:

  • Year-round generation with a monsoon dip, not a brochure that only shows March
  • Ventilated inverter placement, not a sealed cupboard that cooks the electronics
  • Structure and waterproofing that survive a heavy northeast monsoon
  • A realistic cleaning plan — see our panel cleaning and AMC if you already have a plant

What the city paperwork actually is

Most Chennai homes install grid-tied rooftop solar. You keep your EB connection. A net meter records import and export. You still draw power at night. Going fully off-grid with batteries is a different, usually more expensive, project for a typical house — we explain why in going solar does not mean giving up EB.

Eligible homeowners can receive a government subsidy of up to ₹78,000. Subsidy amount and approval follow current scheme rules. If a quote is silent on net metering and subsidy files, ask who owns that work. It is often the delay that sits between “panels installed” and “bill actually lower.”

How to read a Chennai solar quote

Two quotes for the same kilowatts can still be different jobs. Before you sign, line them up on:

  • Scope. Structure, civil work, wiring, earthing, testing, and commissioning in writing.
  • Who supplies what. Full plant versus labour only. Mixing those prices is how homeowners overpay or under-specify.
  • Capacity logic. It should come from your units and usable roof, not “5 kW because everyone takes 5 kW.” Our sizing guide walks through that arithmetic.
  • Aftercare. Who comes when generation drops, and from which part of the city.
