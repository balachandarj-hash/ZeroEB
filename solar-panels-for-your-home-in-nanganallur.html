Before the visit

Three layout questions, then the subsidy.

Do you visit Nanganallur from Madipakkam?

Yes. The roof visit is free. A recent bill is enough to start. You can stop once the drawing is explained. No steel is ordered that day.

The stair room is in the centre. Can modules still fit?

Usually as two short rows, one on each clear side. If only one side is open, we use that side alone. We do not run a long row through the headroom.

A solar water heater is already up there. Must it move?

No. It keeps its corner. Modules take the clear area that is left, and the path to the tank lid stays open.

Which modules qualify for the subsidy?

DCR modules, with cells and modules made in India. Eligible homes can receive up to ₹78,000, subject to the scheme on the day you apply. NDCR is only if that assistance has already been given up.
