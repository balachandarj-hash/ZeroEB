How the Northeast Monsoon Affects Solar Panel Installation Planning in Chennai

Installation guide · 30 October 2026 · 6 min read

October and November bring Chennai's northeast monsoon. For homeowners thinking about solar panel installation in Madipakkam or anywhere in Chennai, this season creates specific practical questions about whether to proceed, how to schedule installation, and what output to expect during the season itself.

Can installation proceed during the monsoon?

Yes. Solar panel installation in Chennai continues through the northeast monsoon. The crew will not work on a wet terrace during active rain, but Chennai monsoon rain comes in spells — typically late afternoon and evening — with clear mornings on many days. A one to two day installation is usually schedulable around the weather. Heavier systems (10 kW and above) with longer installation windows may face a few weather delays, but that rarely extends the total timeline by more than a few days.

What generation looks like in October and November

October and November are lower-generation months in Chennai. Cloud cover on overcast days can cut output to 10–20% of rated capacity. On days with intermittent cloud, the system will produce bursts of near-peak output between clouds. The monthly total for a 3 kW plant in November is typically 240–310 units, compared to 380–420 in March or April. This is seasonal and not a sign that the installation is wrong.

Why the monsoon is not a reason to delay installation

Every month you delay solar panel installation in Madipakkam is a month of full EB bills. Even at reduced November generation, a 3 kW plant still offsets 240+ units, reducing a 400-unit household's bill meaningfully. The months immediately after the monsoon — December, January, February — are good-to-excellent generation months. Delaying installation to January means you paid full bills through November and December.

The best months for solar generation in Chennai are March to May and September to October. The worst are November to January. Installing in October means you catch the tail of the good season before the monsoon ends.

Specific installation considerations in the monsoon

Roof penetrations sealed during monsoon-season installation should use monsoon-rated sealant and be inspected after the first heavy rain. The crew should double-check that all cable glands are properly closed and all junction boxes are sealed. For solar panel installation in Chennai near the coast — including areas like Madipakkam and Pallikaranai, which are close to Pallikaranai marshland — moisture ingress at an open gland can degrade connectors faster than it would in a drier inland climate.

TANGEDCO filing during the monsoon

Net meter application processing at TANGEDCO does not slow significantly in the monsoon. The sub-division offices work through the season. If anything, installer crews are slightly less busy in October and November, which can mean a faster site visit and application filing. For solar panel installation in Madipakkam, file the application on installation day regardless of the season. Waiting until after the monsoon just delays the first bill saving.
