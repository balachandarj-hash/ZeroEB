Common Mistakes in Solar Panel Installation in Chennai

Buying guide · 15 November 2026 · 7 min read

Most regrets after solar panel installation in Chennai come from decisions made before the first panel is lifted — not from the hardware brand on the brochure. These are the mistakes that keep showing up on Madipakkam terraces.

Skipping a real shade check

Water tanks, stairheads, and neighbouring floors throw afternoon shadows that a quick glance at noon will miss. Sizing from Google Earth alone is how plants underperform for twenty-five years. Shade analysis before installation is the fix.

Buying kilowatts instead of a complete scope

The cheapest "5 kW" is often missing structure gauge, earthing detail, or net meter follow-up. You are not buying a number of panels; you are buying a working plant tied to TANGEDCO. Choosing an installer starts with five plain questions.

Ignoring structure and corrosion

Powder-coated mild steel looks fine on day one and rusts in Chennai humidity. Hot-dip galvanising or aluminium costs more upfront and lasts. Ask for the structure specification in the quote, not after the crew has left.

Another frequent miss: assuming the EB bill drops the week the panels go up. Generation can start on commissioning; the bill changes after the net meter swap. When the EB bill changes explains the lag.

Leaving paperwork to "someone later"

Subsidy and net meter filings need matching names, bills, and IDs. Delays here erase months of savings. Keep a document pack ready — the documents checklist covers what TANGEDCO and the portal usually ask for.

No cleaning or service plan
